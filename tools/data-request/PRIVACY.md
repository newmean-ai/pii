# Privacy notice — newmean.ai PII training data requests

Effective 2026-10-31. This notice covers the personal information we process when you request the training and validation data
of the newmean.ai PII project through our request form, and when we carry out the research data license with you. The request
form shows a summary of this notice.

## Who we are

Hoonjae Lee, doing business as newmean.ai (sole proprietorship, Republic of Korea, business registration no. 732-22-02446;
708-B99, 100 Crystal-ro, Seohae-gu, Incheon). Privacy officer: Hoonjae Lee, hoonjae@newmean.ai.

## What we collect

- Your verified Google account email.
- Your answers in the form: name, organization, website, role, country, whether you apply as an individual or for your
  organization, the eligibility confirmations, use case, languages, domains, expected volume, purpose, institution type, planned
  publication, supervisor name, title and email for students, company size and budget for business requests, how you heard
  about us, and your contact preference.
- The license version you accepted, its SHA-256 hash and the time of your acceptance.

We add our review notes (rule checks and the AI-assisted check), the decision, approval and access dates and, if you get
access, a random recipient identifier for your copy of the data.

## Why we use it

- To review your request and to enter into and carry out the data license (Personal Information Protection Act art. 15(1)(4)).
- To protect the data and enforce the license (art. 15(1)(6)).
- To count demand in aggregate.

We contact you about dataset updates only if you choose "Yes" in the last question of the form; you can withdraw that at any
time.

## How long we keep it

- Approved requests: until 5 years after your access ends (the license has terms that continue after access ends).
- Approved requests that never receive access: 1 year after you withdraw or we close the request.
- Rejected or withdrawn requests: 1 year after our decision.

Then we delete them. Aggregate counts that identify no one may be kept.

## Transfers outside Korea

We make these transfers because they are needed to process your request (art. 28-8(1)(3)).

1. **Google** — Google Asia Pacific Pte. Ltd. (Singapore), the Google Workspace contracting entity for a customer billed in
   Korea, and the Google group companies it uses as subprocessors, including Google LLC (USA), in the countries where Google
   has data centres.
   - Items: all of the above.
   - When and how: stored when you submit and whenever we act on your request, over an encrypted network.
   - Purpose: to host the form, the response sheet, the data folder and our email (Google Workspace).
   - Retention: until we delete it as above.
   - Contact: Google's Cloud Data Protection Team for Google Workspace (https://support.google.com/a/contact/googlecloud_dpr;
     that form is for Workspace administrators, so you may also write to us and we will pass your request on).
2. **Anthropic PBC** (USA)
   - Items: the request answers used for the check (purpose, organization, website, role, country, languages, domains,
     volume, institution type, planned publication, company size, budget and use case) and the domain of your email address.
     We do not send your name, your full email address or your supervisor's details.
   - When and how: shortly after you submit, over an encrypted API connection.
   - Purpose: an AI-assisted plausibility check, which may search the public web for your organization.
   - Retention: under Anthropic's policy for API customers, inputs and outputs are deleted from Anthropic's back end within 30
     days, unless a longer period is needed to enforce Anthropic's Usage Policy or to comply with the law. Anthropic does not
     train its models on this data.
   - Contact: privacy@anthropic.com.

A person makes every decision; the AI and rule checks only help. If you do not want your answers sent to Anthropic, do not
submit the form; email hoonjae@newmean.ai instead and we will review your request without the AI step (our email also runs on
Google Workspace).

We do not sell your information or give it to other third parties, except where the law requires.

## Your rights

You may ask to see, correct or delete your information, ask us to stop processing it, or withdraw consent, by emailing
hoonjae@newmean.ai. Deleting the request record of an active license ends your access. You may also complain to the Personal
Information Protection Commission (privacy.go.kr, 182).

## Age

You must be at least 19 years old (or the age of majority where you live, if higher) to apply. We do not accept requests from
minors.

## Changes

If we change this notice, we publish the new version at this address with a new effective date. Earlier versions remain in the
history of this file.
